# Try your first repair

Set aside 10–15 minutes with the included sample. You do not need your own
recordings, an account or paid tools.

[Download the free desktop app](https://github.com/pavangupta352/cleantake/releases/tag/v0.2.0)
for your computer, install it and open CleanTake. It includes the audio tools
and an offline sample. Read the [desktop installation guide](DESKTOP.md) for
supported systems and the current signing limitations: Mac builds are not
notarized, and Windows installers are unsigned. If your computer blocks launch,
follow the guide or report the message; keep your existing protections enabled.
Allow extra time for downloading and installation.

## Explore the sample

1. Open **Sample · recover a missing half-second**. Select the passage near
   **17 seconds**. The sample uses licensed recordings with a deliberately
   introduced gap; see its [attribution and modifications](assets/demo/ATTRIBUTION.md).
2. Listen to **Original**, then **Source** at the same position. Original is
   the main recording; Source lets you audition the alternate microphone.
   Before accepting anything, decide: **would you use this repair, reject it,
   or are you unsure?** Note what you heard and why.
3. Try the edit controls on this disposable sample: choose **Accept repair**,
   then listen in **Repair**. Use **Edit → Undo**, check the decision, and use
   **Edit → Redo**. This exercise is separate from your listening verdict;
   testing acceptance does not mean you liked the sound.
4. Export and save a **WAV** and a **portable project archive** somewhere you
   can find. Locate the WAV and play it. The archive includes the source
   recordings as well as your decisions.
5. Quit CleanTake, reopen it and check that the sample's decision is still
   there. The original recordings remain unchanged.

**Repair plays accepted edits.** While a suggestion is pending, Repair can
sound identical to Original. Use Source to hear the donor before deciding.
The [editing guide](GUIDE.md) explains adjustments and manual repairs.

## Tell us where it worked or stopped

Useful feedback includes:

- CleanTake version, installer filename, operating-system version and computer architecture, if known.
- The last step you completed, where you stopped, and any exact error message.
- Whether you found help yourself or needed someone to explain a control.
- Whether the WAV played and the decision survived reopening.
- Rough time to saved WAV, keeping download and installation time separate.
- Your independent **use / reject / unsure** verdict, with a passage time and reason.

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/pavangupta352/cleantake/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
for problems, or the [recording case form](https://github.com/pavangupta352/cleantake/issues/new?template=recording.yml)
for a reproducible case you have permission to share. Public issues are public:
keep private audio, project archives, access tokens and studio session links out
of them. Check screenshots for personal information before posting. A rejected
repair or an unfinished attempt is useful feedback too.
